# Security Policy

IndiaOne will handle identity verifications, business documents and reports of corruption. Security vulnerabilities can put real people at risk, including people who report bribe demands.

## Reporting a vulnerability
- **Don't open a public issue.**
- Use GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting**: go to the repository's Security tab and choose "Report a vulnerability".
- Include the affected component, steps to reproduce, the impact, and any proof of concept.
- We aim to acknowledge reports within **48 hours** and to agree a fix timeline with you.

## Scope
Everything in this repository, and any deployment the maintainers operate. Government portals and third-party services are **out of scope**. Report issues in government systems to the relevant authority or to CERT-In (incident@cert-in.org.in).

## Safe harbour
We won't pursue action against good-faith research that:
- avoids privacy violations and service disruption;
- doesn't access or alter other people's data;
- gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.

## Commitments
- Incidents affecting a production deployment will be reported to CERT-In within 6 hours, as the CERT-In Directions (2022) require. Affected users and the Data Protection Board will be notified as the DPDP Act and Rules require.
