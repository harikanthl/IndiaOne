## Intent

A launch promo for India Business One, an open-source citizen web app. Built from the site's own
captured screens and art: open on the pain (hidden fees, files stalled on a desk, bribe demands),
answer with the product (address → every licence with the official fee and the law behind it →
track which desk holds your file → report a bribe demand privately), close on the seal and the URL/GitHub.

## Assets

- apps/web/public/brand/seal.webp (+ seal-ring.webp, seal-core.webp) — the logo seal; the ring can turn, the lotus stays upright. Close sting.
- apps/web/public/mascot/tiger.webp, peacock.webp, bush-left.webp, bush-right.webp, jungle-glow.webp — the splash scene layers; the opening/closing world.
- apps/web/public/art/*.jpg — credited Indian paintings used on the site (procession, shops, weavers).
- Site running at http://localhost:3000 (use ?splash=0 to skip the first-visit splash when capturing pages).

## Customizations
