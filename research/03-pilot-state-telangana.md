# Pilot state research and seed rules (as of 14 Oct 2026)

*Research snapshot. **H** means we read the primary source. **M** means several secondary sources agree. **L** means a single source, or sources that conflict. Two official PDFs were not read and need a manual check: GHMC's 2026 trade-licence renewal press note and Tamil Nadu's Trade-UG-2024 fee PDF.*

## 1. Municipal trade licence

### Telangana (recommended)

**GHMC (Hyderabad core)**

- **Portals:**
  - Apply: https://trade.ghmc.gov.in/ (also sa.ghmc.gov.in/TradeNewApplicationEntry.aspx)
  - Pay: onlinepayments.ghmc.gov.in/TLTax/TLPayment
  - Status: ghmc.gov.in/TradeStatus.aspx
  - Licence number (TIN) search: onlinepayments.ghmc.gov.in/TLTax/Search_Your_Trade
  - Ease of Doing Business report: ghmc.gov.in/EODBReport.aspx
- **Process:** fully online. OTP login, form, then payment. The web page says the certificate is issued "instantly with TIN". GHMC's procedure document says the steps are a provisional certificate, field verification, then a digitally signed final certificate.
- **Documents:** ID proof and lease deed or other proof of occupancy (https://ghmc.gov.in/Trade.aspx).
- **Legal basis:**
  - GHMC Act 1955: s.521 with Schedule P (dangerous and offensive trades) and s.622.
  - G.O.Rt.459 of 29.07.2017.
  - GHMC Resolution 173 of 14.11.2019, which removed the fee ceiling.
  - The GHMC page also cites the TS-iPASS Rules (G.O.Ms.50).
- **Possible legal gap (M-L):** one analysis argues that GHMC licensing only covers dangerous and offensive trades. If so, trades not listed in Schedule P would need only Shops & Establishments registration. In practice GHMC licenses general establishments and eating establishments anyway.
- **Fee (M):** charged per square foot, based on the width of the road the premises faces.
  - Road up to 20 ft: ₹3 per sq ft
  - Road 20–30 ft: ₹4 per sq ft
  - Road over 30 ft: ₹6 per sq ft
  - The old caps (₹10k / ₹50k / ₹2L) were reportedly removed in 2019.
  - Official rate PDF, not yet read: ghmc.gov.in/Menu_Files/Trade License Fee rates.pdf
- **Validity and renewal (M):** a licence covers one calendar year. For the 2026 licence year:
  - no penalty until 20 Dec 2025;
  - 25% penalty after that;
  - 50% penalty from 20 Feb 2026.
- **Timeline (L):** a 2017 service list gives 15 days. We found no deemed-approval rule for trade licences. TG-iPASS has deemed approval, but it covers industrial approvals only.
- **Jurisdiction changes:** which corporation covers an address has changed twice in 2025–26.
  - Dec 2025: G.O. 292 merged 27 surrounding urban local bodies (ULBs) into GHMC, giving 300 wards.
  - 10 Feb 2026: G.O.Ms.55 split the enlarged GHMC into three corporations:
    - **GHMC (residual):** 6 zones, 150 wards.
    - **Cyberabad MC:** Serilingampally, Kukatpally and Quthbullapur zones; 76 wards.
    - **Malkajgiri MC:** Malkajgiri, Uppal and LB Nagar zones; 74 wards.
  - The split is being challenged in the High Court.
  - Sources: [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/telangana-govt-reorganises-ghmc-into-three-separate-municipal-corporations-enn26021104131), [South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/telangana/telangana-government-reorganises-ghmc-into-three-separate-municipal-corporations).
  - Not verified: whether the two new corporations still issue licences through trade.ghmc.gov.in.

**Other Telangana ULBs (under the Commissioner and Director of Municipal Administration, CDMA)**

- **Legal basis (H):** Telangana Municipalities Act 2019 (which excludes GHMC) and the Telangana Municipalities Trade Licences (Regulation of Issue and Renewal) Rules 2020 ([LegitQuest](https://www.legitquest.com/act/telangana-municipalities-trade-licenses-regulation-of-issue-and-renewal-rules-2020/D55E)).
- **Fee (Rule 4):** per square foot of plinth area, by road width.
  - Shops and general establishments: ₹3 / ₹4 / ₹5
  - Star hotels and corporate hospitals: ₹6
  - Industrial units: ₹4–7
  - Councils revise rates every 3 years.
- **Process:** the applicant self-certifies. Apply at least 1 week before starting a general trade, or 1 month before a dangerous, offensive or industrial trade. Online issue is "instant as per Schedule-III". Licences are valid to 31 March.
- **Penalties:**
  - Trading without a licence: 25% for the first 3 months, then 50%.
  - Late renewal: 25% until June, 50% from 1 July.
- **Portal:** not verified. cdma.telangana.gov.in returned a 404 error.

### Karnataka

- **Bengaluru (L-M):** the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act took effect on 15 May 2025 and replaced BBMP with five corporations. The Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) issues trade licences centrally. Licences run 1–5 years, and renewal is penalty-free in February. We did not find a fee schedule.
- **Other ULBs:** Karnataka Municipalities Act 1964 and KMC Act 1976, with a common online app required by a DMA circular. We could not confirm Sakala (Karnataka's service-guarantee scheme) timelines for trade licences.

### Tamil Nadu

- **Chennai:** Chennai City Municipal Corporation Act 1919, s.287 with Schedule VI.
  - The council raised fees in 2024, to as much as ₹30k (bakery: ₹500 → ₹2,000). We could not confirm the state approved the hike.
  - Portals: tnurbanepay.tn.gov.in and TN e-Sevai.
- **Business Facilitation Act 2018 (amended 2022):** provides deemed approval and service timelines. Single Window Portal 2.0 renews trade licences immediately.

## 2. Shops & Establishments (S&E) registration

**Telangana (M)**
- **Law:** Telangana Shops & Establishments Act 1988 and Rules 1990. Apply online with Form I.
- **Timeline:** provisional registration within 24 business hours. Valid to 31 December; renewal is automatic via Form IV.
- **Fee, by number of workers:**

  | Workers | Fee |
  |---|---|
  | 0 | ₹100 |
  | 1–5 | ₹500 |
  | 6–10 | ₹1,000 |
  | 11–20 | ₹2,000 |
  | 21–50 | ₹5,000 |

- **Recent orders:**
  - G.O.Rt.383 (23 Sep 2025) exempts establishments with 10 or fewer employees from most of the Act. Registration and renewal still apply.
  - G.O.Rt.282 (5 Jul 2025) allows a 10-hour working day.
- **Portal:** labour.telangana.gov.in

**OSH Code**
- Registration is required for establishments with 10 or more workers.
- Maharashtra (30 Apr 2026) and Haryana (4 May 2026) dropped their S&E registration to avoid duplicating it ([Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2026-05/ERGO-registration-of-Establishment-under-OSH-Code.pdf)).
- Telangana has made no such change yet. **Watch item.**

**Karnataka**
- Shops & Commercial Establishments (Amendment) Act 2026, notified 4 Sep 2026.
- What changed:
  - registration is fully digital;
  - registration no longer needs renewal;
  - registration is deemed granted after 7 days unless the inspector gives reasons for refusal;
  - establishments with 10+ workers that are registered under the OSH Code are excluded.

**Tamil Nadu (H)**
- Only establishments with 10 or more workers register: Form Y, ₹100 fee, Form Z certificate within 24 hours.
- Smaller establishments file Form ZB instead.
- Source: [TN Gazette](https://stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2024/217_Ex_III_1a_2024.pdf).

## 3. FSSAI food licensing (national; changed in 2026)

- **New thresholds (H):** set by the FSS (Licensing & Registration) Amendment of 10 Mar 2026 and FSSAI order F.No.RCD-01002/1/2021 of 13 Mar 2026, **effective 1 Apr 2026** ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1031736)).

  | Annual turnover | What you need |
  |---|---|
  | Up to ₹1.5 cr (previously ₹12 lakh) | Registration |
  | ₹1.5 cr – ₹50 cr | State licence |
  | Over ₹50 cr, or specified kinds of business (importers, e-commerce, others) | Central licence |

- **Validity:** registrations and licences no longer expire, but the annual fee is still payable. Missing the fee or the annual return means deemed suspension.
- **Fees (M):** registration ₹100 a year. State licence ₹2,000–5,000 (the portal reportedly charges a flat ₹5,000). Central licence ₹7,500.
- **Timelines:**
  - Registration: granted, or an inspection ordered, within 7 days. If nothing happens within 30 days, registration is deemed granted.
  - Licence: up to 60 days.
  - "Tatkal" instant grant for non-manufacturing businesses since Jul 2024.
- **Bakeries:** a bakery is likely classed as manufacturing, so Tatkal probably doesn't apply. Not confirmed: whether the 100 kg/day manufacturing cap for registration survives the 2026 amendment.
- **Portal:** https://foscos.fssai.gov.in. No official API. A public business search exists but is behind a CAPTCHA. Third parties (Surepass, Gridlines) sell verification APIs.

## 4. Other approvals that may apply (Telangana)

- **Fire NOC (L-M):**
  - Telangana Fire Service Act 1999: a NOC is needed for buildings taller than 15 m. A 2025–26 revision reportedly raised this to 24 m for residential buildings only.
  - Who issues it: the Regional Fire Officer for buildings 10–15 m, the District Fire Officer for buildings under 10 m. Service charter timeline: 7 days.
  - Area or seating triggers for shops and restaurants not verified.
- **Pollution control:**
  - Retail shops: not applicable.
  - Bakeries: Green or Orange category depending on capacity.
  - MoEFCC White list (17 Oct 2025): covers bakeries of 0.5–1 TPD that use only electricity or gas and discharge into a sewer.
  - Telangana pollution board circular 94/2025 adds more White-category activities. Its text was not seen.
- **Signage:**
  - GHMC permission for name boards and advertisements is online, with a fee you calculate yourself per square metre. Applications go to circle offices (since 27 Nov 2025).
  - G.O.Ms.68/2020: board no wider than 15% of the frontage, no flashing lights, nothing above 15 ft.
- **Professional Tax (M):** Telangana Tax on Professions Act 1987.
  - Monthly deduction per employee, by salary:

    | Monthly salary | Tax per month |
    |---|---|
    | Up to ₹15,000 | Nil |
    | ₹15,001 – ₹20,000 | ₹150 |
    | Over ₹20,000 | ₹200 |

  - The business itself enrols for ₹2,500 a year. Returns are due by the 10th of each month.
- **GST (M-H):** Telangana kept the **₹20 lakh threshold for goods**; services are also ₹20 lakh. Karnataka and Tamil Nadu use ₹40 lakh for goods.
- **Udyam:** free, Aadhaar-based, and optional.
- **Legal Metrology:**
  - Weighing scales must be verified and stamped. Portal: https://tglm.cgg.gov.in
  - Businesses that sell their own pre-packed goods need packer (LMPC) registration, about ₹500 (not verified).

## Recommendation: pilot in Telangana

**Why Telangana**
1. The founder is in Hyderabad, so visiting ULB offices and checking rules in person is practical.
2. ULBs under the Municipalities Act use one statewide published fee formula (Rules 2020: rate per sq ft by road width), which is easy to encode.
3. GHMC is fully online, with public status and licence-number lookups.
4. Telangana is a BRAP 2024 Top Achiever in 4 reform areas.
5. The 2025–26 merger and three-way split shows the core problem the product solves: which authority covers this address? It also means real ongoing work keeping our jurisdiction data current.

**Pilot ULBs**
1. **GHMC (residual core):** mature digital process.
2. **Cyberabad MC:** new in Feb 2026 and dense with cafés and startups. Tests whether we map addresses to the right corporation and handle the move off the old GHMC portal.
3. **A smaller municipality under the TM Act 2019:** Siddipet or Karimnagar MC. Tests the CDMA rules path.
