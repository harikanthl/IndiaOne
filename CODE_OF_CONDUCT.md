# Code of Conduct

IndiaOne brings together people from every state, language, religion, caste, gender, profession and background to build something for all Indians. We expect everyone in the project's spaces to treat each other with respect.

## Expected behaviour
- Be kind, patient and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and people for whom English is not their first language.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy, both of contributors and of the citizens and officials we talk about.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, intimidation, slurs, or discrimination of any kind, including on caste, religion, region, language, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
- Doxxing, or publishing anyone's personal information.
- **Naming or accusing individual government officials** in project spaces. Report allegations to the competent authorities (ACB, Lokayukta, vigilance) instead.
- Partisan political campaigning. This project is non-partisan.
- Using the project to promote paid "fast-track" or agent services.

## Enforcement
Report concerns to the maintainers privately (contact details are in the repository profile). Maintainers can remove content and suspend or ban participants. Decisions are made fairly and are documented.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
